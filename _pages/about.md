About Me
======
My name is Anne Ju Laberge. I am a PhD student in [Prof. Eli Strauss lab](https://straussed.github.io/) at the Integrative Biology Department at Michigan State University. My project will look at socio-spatial behavior and demographic changes in wild spotted hyenas in response to an increasing anthropogenic presence. I spent the last year (2025) collecting behavioral data on spotted hyenas in the spectacular Maasai Mara National Reserve in Kenya, which is the long-term data set I will be using for my PhD project.

I graduated from my M. Sc. degree in August 2024 from Université de Sherbrooke (UdeS), Canada. I did my project under the supervision of [Prof. Fanie Pelletier](https://labopelletier.weebly.com/), full professor at UdeS, and co-supervised by Dr. Xavier Bordeleau, researcher at the Department of Fisheries and Oceans Canada.

[My master's project](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390941421_Croissance_neonatale_des_chiots_phoques_communs_de_l'estuaire_du_Saint-Laurent) aimed to characterize the neonatal growth patterns of harbour seal pups (Phoca vitulina) living in the St. Lawrence Estuary and evaluate the different factors on spatial and temporal scales that affect their growth. 

I am interested in animal behaviour, animal movement, and conservation. I am interested in questions on individual and population level. I am also very interested in unravelling the impact of human disturbances on wildlife.
